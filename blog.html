Process

How I approach technical writing

My writing process is meant to turn engineering experience into clear and useful communication.

01

Choose a real backend topic

Start from practical engineering problems instead of abstract theory.

02

Explain the problem clearly

Make the reader understand why the topic matters in real systems.

03

Present a practical structure

Show how architecture, patterns, or design choices solve the problem.

04

Keep the explanation readable

Make technical depth understandable without losing engineering quality.
